Our Heritage

A Family Legacy, One Frame at a Time

Established January 30, 1989

Rooted in La Paz, Iloilo City

Gina’s Photo & Video Services began over 37 years ago on January 30, 1989, in Brgy. Rizal, La Paz, Iloilo City[cite: 3, 4]. What started as a dedicated door-to-door family photography venture grew through perseverance, passion, and genuine care for the local community[cite: 3, 8].

Led by founders Gina P. Lachica and Erwin A. Portades, the studio has evolved with modern photography and videography equipment while preserving the warmth and personal touch that families, students, and schools in Iloilo City trust[cite: 3, 8].
