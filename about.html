Our Story

A family business, built one memory at a time

Gina's Photo & Video Services began on January 30, 1989 as a family-owned photography business in Iloilo City. It was founded by Gina P. Lachica, and today it is run together with Erwin A. Portades.

Since then, we have been part of the celebrations, milestones, and everyday occasions of Ilonggo families, from weddings and graduations to birthdays and family gatherings. Our goal is simple: to make sure the moments that matter are kept safe and beautifully told.
